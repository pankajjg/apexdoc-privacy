Privacy Policy

ApexDoc: PDF Scanner & OCR · Effective date: 26 September 2026

ApexDoc ("the app") is a document scanner and PDF tool. This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short: your documents stay on your device.

Information the app handles

  • Documents and scans. PDFs you scan, import or open, and images you pick from your gallery, are stored only in the app's storage on your device. The app does not upload them to any server operated by us.
  • Recognized text (OCR). Text read from your documents is created on your device and saved in the app's private storage so you can search your documents. It is never sent to us.
  • Camera. The camera is used only to scan documents. Photos are not sent to us.
  • Settings. Your preferences (text size, file naming, date format) are stored on your device.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect your name, email address, location, contacts or device identifiers.
  • We do not use advertising or our own analytics services, and we do not sell or share personal data.
  • We do not create user accounts.

Third-party components

  • Google ML Kit (document scanning and text recognition) processes your documents on your device. ML Kit may send anonymous technical and performance data (such as error and usage statistics, never your document content) to Google, and some components are delivered and updated by Google Play services. See Google's privacy information at policies.google.com/privacy.
  • Sharing. When you choose to share or send a PDF (for example by email or WhatsApp), the file goes to the app you select. That app's own privacy policy applies.

Storage and deletion

Documents and recognized text remain on your device until you delete them. Deleted documents stay in the Recycle Bin for 30 days and are then permanently removed. Uninstalling the app removes its private data.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this address with a new effective date.
